Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your next adventure.

What is included in the adventure package price?

All packages include certified guides, safety equipment, accommodation, meals as specified, transport during the trip, and comprehensive travel insurance. Optional add-ons like photography services are available.

What fitness level do I need for adventures?

We offer adventures for every fitness level. Each trip is rated Easy, Moderate, or Hard. Check the difficulty guide on our Adventures page, or contact us for a personalized recommendation.

Can I book a custom or private adventure?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom itineraries for individuals, families, and corporate groups. Contact us with your preferences and we'll design a unique adventure just for you.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 30 days before departure. 50% refund for cancellations 15-30 days before. No refund within 14 days, but you can reschedule to another date at no charge.

Do you provide equipment, or should I bring my own?

All specialized safety equipment is provided. For personal items like hiking boots, backpacks, and clothing, we'll send you a detailed packing list after booking. Rentals are available for most gear.

Are solo travelers welcome?

Of course! Many of our travelers come solo. You can join a group trip to meet fellow adventurers, or book a private guided experience. We also offer solo traveler discounts on select trips.
