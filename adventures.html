Know Before You Go

Adventure Difficulty Levels

Find the right adventure for your fitness and experience level.

Easy

Beginner Friendly

Perfect for first-timers and families. No prior experience needed. Light physical activity, mostly flat terrain.

  • No experience required
  • Suitable for ages 10+
  • Light physical effort
Moderate

Some Experience

For those with basic fitness and some outdoor experience. Moderate physical activity, varied terrain.

  • Basic fitness recommended
  • Suitable for ages 14+
  • 3-5 hours activity
Hard

Expert Level

For experienced adventurers only. High fitness required. Challenging terrain and extreme conditions.

  • Strong fitness required
  • Suitable for ages 18+
  • 6+ hours activity
